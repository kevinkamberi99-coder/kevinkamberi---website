About

Research built to be checked, not just read.

I'm an independent researcher covering the macro cycle, sector fundamentals, and portfolio construction — publishing the reasoning behind each view, not just the headline call.

KK

I started publishing independent research after several years on the buy side, where I found that the most useful work was rarely the loudest call — it was the note that showed its data, stated the conditions under which it would be wrong, and was revised in public when the evidence changed.

That's the standard I try to hold this site to. Every publication carries a working paper number and a date, so a view can be tracked — and challenged — over time.

What I cover

My work sits across three connected layers: the macro backdrop that sets the regime, the sector and company fundamentals that sit inside it, and the portfolio construction question of how a view should actually change position sizing and hedging.

How each note is put together

Abstract

State the claim plainly

One paragraph, written so the conclusion can be understood — and disagreed with — before reading further.

Key findings

Make it checkable

Specific, falsifiable findings rather than narrative, so the underlying data can be verified independently.

Discussion

Engage the objections

The strongest counter-arguments are addressed directly, along with the conditions that would change the view.

Background

Before publishing independently, I worked in investment research and portfolio analytics roles spanning macro strategy and equity sector coverage. That background shapes the framing here: research is treated as an input to a portfolio decision, not an end in itself.
